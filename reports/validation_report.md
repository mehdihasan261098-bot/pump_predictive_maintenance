## Interpretation and limitations

* Detection delay is dominated by physics: a fault must change the measured signals beyond the healthy noise and operating-point variability (e.g. overheating is limited by the 10-min bearing thermal time constant).
* Simulator, features, rules and ML share the same modelling assumptions; results on synthetic data **do not establish industrial diagnostic accuracy**. Real deployment requires machine-specific baselines and field data.
